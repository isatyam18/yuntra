# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Yuntra community respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, professional, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differing technical viewpoints and lived experience.
- Report security concerns privately rather than publishing exploit details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this code is violated. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the repository's contact channel. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
